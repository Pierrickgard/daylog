# Getting help

- **Something doesn't work**: open a [bug report](https://github.com/Pierrickgard/daylog/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)
  with your Daylog and macOS versions.
- **An idea or a question**: open a [feature request](https://github.com/Pierrickgard/daylog/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)
  or a [blank issue](https://github.com/Pierrickgard/daylog/issues/new).
- **A security issue**: don't open an issue, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Daylog is maintained on spare time, so answers may take a few days.
